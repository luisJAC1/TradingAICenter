The Decision Inbox — Step by Step

This is the only action you need to take daily. Everything else is automatic.
1️⃣
See the badge increment
The 🧭 button in the header shows a number (e.g. 🧭 1). That means a Boss-approved trade signal is waiting for you.
2️⃣
Click "Pending Decisions"
The modal opens. You'll see a card for each pending item with the agent avatar, signal type, and timestamp.
3️⃣
Read the signal content
The box in the middle of each card shows the full signal: ticker, direction (LONG/SHORT), conviction %, entry price, stop-loss, TP1/TP2/TP3, risk %, thesis, and the Eleventh Man's counter-argument.
4️⃣
Click a numbered option button
At the bottom of the card you'll see buttons like 1. Approve, 2. Reject, maybe 3. Wait 1h. Click the number for your choice. The button turns "Sending..." while it processes, then the item disappears from the inbox.
5️⃣
(Optional) Talk to the agent first
Each inbox item has an "Open Chat" button. Click it to open a direct chat with that agent and ask questions before deciding. The chat lets you type freely — the agent responds in context.
✅
On Approve → Trigger executes the paper trade
Trigger places a market order + GTC stop-loss via Alpaca paper trading. Watchdog picks up the position immediately and starts monitoring every 5 minutes. Historian logs it. The system is now tracking a live paper position.
"No pending decisions right now" = agents haven't generated a signal yet, or all signals have been handled. The Architect runs every 4 hours. Check back later or watch the Office view to see when signals are building.

"Options are being prepared..." = Boss is still scoring. Hit Refresh after 60 seconds.

Signal Flow — Research to Your Inbox
How a market event becomes a trade signal in your inbox
🔍
Phase 1 — Research (9 agents, 15min–4h)
Charts scans technicals every 15min. Headlines analyzes news impact every 15min. Globe monitors macro regime every 30min. Cryptid watches on-chain every 30min. Ape tracks Reddit sentiment every 30min. Recon finds unusual options activity every 1h. Accountant runs fundamentals every 4h. All results publish to the Redis Knowledge Bus.
📊
Phase 2 — Analysis (5 agents, 15min + on-demand)
Mood Ring fuses all research into a unified sentiment score (−100 to +100) every 15min. Pattern Master rates setups 1–5 stars every 15min. Bridge tracks correlation regime breaks every 30min. When Architect needs a debate, Bull and Bear are triggered on-demand to make the strongest case each way.
🏗️
Phase 3 — Strategy (The Architect, 4h cycle)
Scores candidates by: source count × confidence × pattern hit × mood hit. Requests Bull + Bear cases for top candidates (waits 45s for both). Claude synthesizes a trade plan — entry, stop, TPs, risk %, R:R, timeframe, full thesis, and plays the Eleventh Man internally before publishing. Scribe formats it for your inbox.
🛡️
Phase 4 — Risk Gatekeeping (Shield → Eleventh Man → Boss)
Shield runs 7 hard rules (no LLM, pure math): risk %, portfolio heat, plan count, duplicate ticker, correlated assets, pre-event flags. If it passes, Eleventh Man generates a mandatory contrarian counter-argument. Boss then scores 0–100 using conviction, stars, mood, regime, and R:R. Score 50–64 gets a Haiku LLM review. Score below 50 = SKIP.
🧭
Phase 5 — Your Inbox (Messenger, 2h window)
Messenger delivers the Boss-approved signal to your UI Decision Inbox (and optionally WhatsApp). You have 2 hours to approve or reject. Auto-cancels if no action.
⚡
Phase 6 — Execution (Trigger + Watchdog)
On your APPROVE: Trigger calculates position size (risk_dollars / stop_distance), places market order, places GTC stop immediately after. Watchdog picks it up and monitors every 5 minutes: stop hits, TP1/2/3 progression, flash crashes, emergency drawdown.
📚
Phase 7 — Learning (Historian + Professor)
Historian records every closed trade to SQLite: entry, exit, P&L, close_type, conviction, stars, mood score, regime, full plan JSON. Professor publishes weekly: agent leaderboard (who's signals win?), post-mortem (what worked, what failed, system suggestions).

The Seven Non-Negotiables
Design principles that will not change
  • 1
    Human approves every trade. AI recommends, you decide. The system cannot trade without your explicit click. Always.
  • 2
    Knowledge Bus is the nervous system. No agent works in a silo. Every signal, debate, and veto flows through Redis so all agents share context.
  • 3
    The Eleventh Man must be heard. Every high-conviction plan gets a mandatory contrarian challenge before Boss approval. Prevents groupthink.
  • 4
    Paper trading first. 30+ days minimum before considering real money. No exceptions. LIVE_TRADING is hardlocked false in code.
  • 5
    Tokin enforces the budget. No LLM call can bankrupt the system. Hard veto at 100% of monthly budget.
  • 6
    UI first for notifications. WhatsApp is opt-in, not required. The Decision Inbox in the UI is always the primary channel.
  • 7
    Zero LLM where logic suffices. Shield, Watchdog, Trigger, Historian, Tokin, Messenger, Scribe — pure code. LLM reserved for reasoning tasks only.
